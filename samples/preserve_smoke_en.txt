Senior Wang stood beside Wang Lin at the gate of the Ji Realm.

"Fellow Daoist Wang, the Host is waiting for us," said Senior Brother Chen.

The host of the roadside inn bowed when they entered, but the Host did not move.

Elder Zhou carried the Heaven Defying Bead while disciples of the Heavenly Fate Sect watched from the courtyard.

Wang Lin looked at Wang and then at Chen. None of the three names changed, even though the titles and descriptive terms around them belonged to the language of the sentence.
